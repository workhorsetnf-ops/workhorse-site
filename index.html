You're training hard and looking the same

More on the background

The questions I actually get asked

I'm not signed anywhere. Is this for people at my level?

Yes, and most of the roster talent I work with were exactly where you are when they started. The programming doesn't change based on where you're booked. What changes is how much travel we're working around, and right now you probably have more control over your week than they do. That's an advantage.

I'm not a wrestler at all. I just want to look like one.

Then this is more straightforward for you, not less. You sleep in your own bed and can train on a real schedule, so we're not building around bumps and drives. Same nutrition system, same training logic, fewer constraints.

I travel constantly. Won't I just fall off again?

That's the thing this is built for. The program assumes you'll miss sessions, eat badly some days, and sleep in strange rooms. Nothing in it depends on a perfect week, because nobody gets perfect weeks. If you fall off, we adjust the plan instead of pretending you'll try harder.

I've had a coach before. It didn't work.

Ask yourself whether they ever changed anything. Most coaching is a program sent once and a check-in nobody reads closely. I read every check-in myself and I adjust from what you actually reported. If something isn't working I'd rather change it in week two than defend it for twelve.

How long before I see something?

Photos start looking different around week six for most people. The scale moves before that, but it lies during a travel week, so I'd rather you judge it on photos and on your working weights holding.

Do I need a full gym?

No. A commercial gym is easiest and that's what most weeks look like. Hotel gyms and minimal equipment get substitutions built in from day one, because I'm writing around the same problem.

Am I too old for this?

I'm 25 years into taking bumps for a living and still doing it. Recovery gets managed differently after 35, which means the program looks different, not smaller.

What does it cost?

We cover that on the call, after I know what your week looks like and whether I can actually help. If I'm not the right fit I'll tell you and point you somewhere better.
