My story

25 years of getting back up

I've loved wrestling for as long as I can remember. I went from backyard wrestling with my friends, to a wrestling school in Georgia, to living the dream on the independent scene.

It wasn't all good. I made a lot of mistakes. I stayed in Georgia, I didn't travel, and I didn't push myself to get better. I was stuck there for years.

When I finally got out, I started working better promotions in neighboring states. My first real break was Evolve under Gabe Sapolsky. That's where I started to see myself as a star and learned what it would take to get there.

In 2021, at 37, I signed with WWE. I was released about six months later. The day my non-compete ended, I wrestled for AEW, and I've been there ever since.

After 25 years, I've made most of the mistakes you can make in this business. I coach younger wrestlers so they don't have to make them too. That's my mission.
