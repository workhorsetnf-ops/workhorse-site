Pricing & terms

Main Event Ready coaching

What you're buying, what it costs, how you can pay, and what happens if you need to stop. Read this before you sign up.

Who provides the coaching

Coaching is provided by Anthony Henry, trading as Workhorse Training & Nutrition. Questions or support: anthony@workhorse-training.com.

What's included

  • A free Game Plan Call before you buy, where we go through your schedule, shows, travel, gym access and current eating.
  • Custom training, macros and cardio delivered in the Workhorse Strong app, with hotel-gym swaps for road weeks.
  • Weekly check-ins (weight, photos, training feedback) that I review myself, with your plan adjusted as needed.
  • Weekly group calls and a monthly one-on-one call.
  • Access to the Workhorse Skool community and its lessons.

How long it lasts

Main Event Ready is a 120-day coaching program. It starts on the day your plan is delivered in the app.

Price

Coaching starts from $400 a month. Packages are tailored to each client, so the final price is agreed with you on your Game Plan Call and confirmed in writing before you pay anything.

How you can pay

  • Upfront: pay for the full 120-day program in one payment.
  • Monthly: pay in monthly instalments across the program.
  • Buy now, pay later: financing is available through Affirm at checkout, subject to Affirm's approval. If you finance with Affirm, your repayment terms are set by Affirm and agreed between you and Affirm.

Payments are processed securely through Newie.

Refunds

  • If you cancel before your plan has been delivered in the app, you get a full refund.
  • Once your plan has been delivered, payments are non-refundable, because the coaching has been built for you and the work has started.

Commitment and cancellation

  • Coaching is a 120-day commitment.
  • If you pay monthly and stop early, the remaining payments for the 120-day program are still owed.
  • If you paid upfront or financed through Affirm, stopping early does not trigger a refund for the unused time.
  • To stop coaching, email anthony@workhorse-training.com. No set notice period is needed.
  • Nothing renews automatically at the end of the 120 days. If you want to keep going, we agree a new package together.

Questions

If anything here isn't clear, email anthony@workhorse-training.com or ask on your Game Plan Call before you buy.
