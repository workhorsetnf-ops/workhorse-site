Before you lock up, before your first move, people have already watched you make your entrance. Then the bell rings and you need the strength, speed, agility and gas tank to deliver the match.

The main programme

Training splits built for pro wrestlers, a new one every six weeks

Every training day covers plyos, power, strength, hypertrophy and conditioning, so you work on your physique, strength, speed, agility and gas tank together. Pick your track, set the days you can train, and log every set in the Workhorse Strong app.
