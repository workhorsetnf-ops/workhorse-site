The main programme

Training splits built for pro wrestlers. A new one every six weeks.

Your look gets judged before your first move. Then you need the strength, speed, agility and gas tank to deliver the match. The training is built for both.

Every training day covers plyos, power, strength, hypertrophy and conditioning. Off days have Bulletproof work to keep you durable, plus optional zone 2. You train 4 days a week in a regular gym, on the days you choose, and you can move a session when your week changes. Every set gets logged in the Workhorse Strong app.

Track 1

Main Event Build

Stronger, more explosive and in better shape for the ring. Built around the squat, bench, deadlift and overhead press, with jumps, throws and conditioning every week.

Track 2

Women's Track

Glutes, hamstrings, strength and a full gas tank. 2 upper and 2 lower days built around hip thrusts, deadlifts, bench and presses, with jumps and conditioning.

Questions

Who is this for?

Rising pro wrestlers who want to improve how they look and how they perform in the ring, and who'll follow a plan on their own. You'll still need to put the work in. Joining is the start.

Is this one-on-one coaching?

No. Uprising is a self-run programme with a community around it. Custom macros, coaching calls, weekly check-ins with me and form reviews are part of my one-on-one coaching, which you can book a call about here.

What do I need?

A regular gym 4 days a week and a phone for the app. On the road, the Road Warrior Protocol gives you a swap for every exercise when the hotel gym is missing something.

I work a job and wrestle on weekends. Will it fit?

You choose which days you train, and you can move a session to another day when the week changes. Shows, work and travel go in first and training fits around them.

How do I cancel?

From the billing screen in the app, any time. You won't be charged again.
